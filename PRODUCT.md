# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential employers, clients, and collaborators visiting the site to evaluate skills, experience, and fit. They arrive with a specific goal: quickly assess whether my work and style align with their needs.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes a memorable, positive impression. It showcases technical capability, design sensibility, and professional history through an engaging single-page experience. Success is a visitor wanting to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Technical, Sharp, Friendly — "Pastel Cyber" / "Soft Tech". A unique contrast between sharp, robotic typography and a soft, welcoming pastel palette. The UI feels like a friendly but highly advanced machine. Light mode by default.

## Anti-references

- Overly dark / gamer aesthetic: no default dark mode, no neon overload, no gaming-style UI

## Design Principles

1. **Make it personal, not a template** — The site should feel uniquely mine, not like a generic SaaS or portfolio template.
2. **First impression counts** — The hero section must immediately communicate who I am and what I do, with a distinct visual hook.
3. **Show, don't tell** — Let the design and interactivity demonstrate skill rather than just listing it.
4. **Clarity over cleverness** — Navigation, hierarchy, and calls to action must be immediately understood.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices: adequate contrast, keyboard navigable, semantic HTML, responsive design. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.
